# Notes

Notes are shared Markdown documents you can read and edit in DreamLake or from
your terminal. Choose a guide for the task at hand.

## Basics

- [Create and find notes](notes-getting-started.md) — login, search, create, and first read.
- [Reading and changes](notes-reading.md) — sections, passage search, and change history.
- [Editing with patches](notes-editing.md) — targeted edits that preserve concurrent work.
- [Live collaboration](notes-collaboration.md) — task identity, presence, and following edits.
- [Saved versions](notes-versions.md) — immutable checkpoints and retained history.
- [Legacy commands](notes-legacy.md) — older ETag-based scripts.

## Rich content

- [Markdown and highlights](notes-rich-content.md) — text color, references, and HTML snapshots.
- [Videos](notes-videos.md) — upload a clip and embed its player.
- [Columns and rows](notes-columns.md) — two-column blocks and repeated pairs.
- [Attachments](notes-attachments.md) — upload, manage, and preview files.
- [Suggested-edit redlines](notes-suggest-diff.md) — propose document changes for review.

## Management

- [Sharing and visibility](notes-sharing.md) — links, access roles, and public/private visibility.
- [Project filing](notes-projects.md) — create and inspect project affiliations.
- [Ownership transfers](notes-transfers.md) — preview and transfer a standalone Note.
- [Delete a note](notes-deletion.md) — remove a whole Note.
